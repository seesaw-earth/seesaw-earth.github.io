RodentFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

RodentFlow is developed by Ziqing Shi as a research utility for rodent behavioral video analysis.

1. Data Collection

RodentFlow does not collect personal information or user data. No account or sign-in is required.

RodentFlow does not use advertising SDKs, analytics services, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection services.

2. Videos and Photos

RodentFlow allows users to select videos from Photos or Files for behavioral analysis. Selected videos are processed locally on the user's device.

RodentFlow does not upload experimental videos, images, or video-derived tracking data to a developer-operated server or cloud service.

3. Experiment Information

Users may enter experiment information such as mouse ID, experimental group, date, and arena dimensions. This information is stored locally as part of experiment results and is not transmitted to the developer.

4. Analysis Results

Tracking results and associated experiment information may be saved locally on the device and exported by the user in JSON format. Exported files remain under the user's control.

5. Network Access

RodentFlow does not require an internet connection to perform video tracking or behavioral analysis.

6. Third-Party Services

RodentFlow does not include third-party advertising or analytics services that collect user data.

7. Children's Privacy

RodentFlow is intended as a scientific research and educational utility and is not designed specifically for children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if RodentFlow's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page.
