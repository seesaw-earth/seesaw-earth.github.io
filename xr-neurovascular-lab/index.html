Case 01 · Version 0.2

Navigation Under UncertaintySuperior-division access with mixed-reality guidance

Advance a shaped guidewire from the parent artery to the planned target in the superior division. Live fluoroscopy and a registered 3D reconstruction are available. You decide what to trust.

  1. 1Main trunk. Get used to the instrument. At the first fork, go up and to the right, not left.
  2. 2Rightward segment (anterior division). At the second fork it overlaps a backward branch on AP; check LAT or the 3D model.
  3. 3First upward branch. A small side branch. Do not enter it.
  4. 4Between the two upward branches. A short straight piece.
  5. 5Second upward branch (superior division). Turn up into it and stop at the ring.

New in this version: the roadmap mask can go stale when the patient moves, a model-based navigation assist offers advice, and the displays will pause briefly to ask where you think the wire is. You can acquire a new roadmap or verify the registration at any time. Both cost time and contrast.
