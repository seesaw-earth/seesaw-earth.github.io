About

I trained as a physician and neuroscientist, and now work as a research engineer at Yale School of Medicine. My work turns raw video and sensor streams into measurements that clinicians and engineers can trust.

  • Markerless 3D human motion. Pose, mesh, and joint kinematics from everyday mobile and clinical video.
  • Multimodal sensing. Video, IMU, motion capture, gaze, EEG/BCI, and task events on one synchronized timeline.
  • Surgical video in 3D. Camera pose, depth, and point maps from procedural video, toward robotic perception.
  • Deployable GPU systems. Taking research models from a laptop to reproducible AWS and CUDA pipelines.

Projects

Markerless 3D human motion analysis

Pipelines that turn mobile and clinical video into 3D human meshes, joint trajectories, and posture and asymmetry metrics. Applied to early neuromotor assessment in newborns and motor stereotypies in autism, and validated with two-camera agreement and joint-level correlations.

  • HSMR
  • PyTorch
  • OpenCV
  • Detectron2

Cloud GPU pipeline for long video

A private S3 to GPU to S3 workflow with reproducible environments, structured outputs, logging, and automated quality checks. Handles continuous recordings up to 24 hours and more than 2.5 million frames.

  • AWS EC2/S3
  • CUDA
  • Linux
  • FFmpeg

Multimodal human-state sensing

Synchronized first- and third-person cameras with IMU, motion capture, gaze, and body and hand pose. Timestamp alignment, calibration, and cross-sensor agreement checks, with video-derived motion validated against OptiTrack.

  • Video
  • IMU
  • OptiTrack
  • Gaze
  • EEG/BCI

Surgical video to 3D

Recovering camera pose, intrinsics, depth, and dense point maps from procedural video, and mapping where methods break: tissue deformation, specular glare, occlusion, blur, and weak parallax.

  • 3D reconstruction
  • Depth
  • Point maps

Video understanding with vision-language models

Turning procedural and simulation video into time-stamped actions and reviewable evidence for human-in-the-loop evaluation.

  • VLM
  • Simulation

Interactive experiment systems

Unity3D VR and CAVE applications with experiment state machines and synchronized logging, plus Arduino-based apparatus with sensors and real-time control.

  • Unity3D / C#
  • Arduino
  • VR
